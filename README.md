## A Journey of Resilience, Accelerated Learning, and Software Innovation

Navigating multiple economic and personal turning points, I have forged a solid career as a software engineer built on autonomy, rapid adaptation, and a constant pursuit of excellence, my trajectory is not a traditional, linear path. Instead, it is defined by an relentless drive for self-improvement, a genuine passion for technology, and a core philosophy I carry into everything I do: <br/>
### **"There is no such thing in the world that I don't know that I can't learn."** 

### *Early Roots: Digital Culture and a Competitive Edge*

Born in 1989, technology has always been a fundamental part of my environment. Growing up immersed in early computers and the internet, I developed a strong capacity for self-directed learning. By myself, I mastered video, audio, and image editing, as well as Spanish. In 2003, during my teens, I studied at a Technical High School of Information Technology. There, I consistently achieved the highest grades in programming logic, building a structured analytical foundation.
The need to master the English language—which is now crucial to my career—arose organically from a competitive passion. Like every teenager, i spent nearly every day playing games, in my case first-person shooter (FPS) games. When I turned 15, i suggested to a friend and I form a team to compete nationally.
As professional gaming was not yet established at the time, and that the most competitive environments were active in the United States, I decided to join American servers. Despite not knowing a single word of English, I spent hours every day with American players on TeamSpeak. I actively absorbed game expressions, asked questions, and learned vocabulary through immersion. This autodidactic approach not only made me fluent in English but also led me to captain teams that won multiple Brazilian tournaments and an open US competition.

### *Defours, Detours, and Testing Resilience*

After high school, I completed a Java SE course, but the missteps of youth temporarily pulled me away from software. I entered Civil Engineering college, during which I was introduced to network marketing. Captivated by the prospect of building financial independence, I dedicated years to sales and leadership development, even taking courses based on Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP). While this path ultimately cost me the chance to finish my engineering degree, it drastically refined my interpersonal, communication, and leadership skills.
In the following years, as a dedicated father providing for a partner and three children, I took on diverse roles to fulfill my parenting responsibilities. I worked as a cook assistant, a waiter, and for nearly three years, an app driver. Working exhausting 12-to-16-hour days, my technological mind never shut down; I was constantly flooded with ideas for applications to help drivers and grew deeply interested in game development.
Seeking stability, I spent my limited gaps between passengers studying for public contests. In 2019, I also began a Law degree at Fadergs University to further support this goal. I successfully passed the public examination to serve as a Municipal Civil Guard in Gravataí-RS, but macroeconomic shifts froze the civil servant call-ups. When the 2020 pandemic triggered a strict lockdown, I lost the car I was renting and my livelihood as a driver. Facing unemployment and severe personal distress, I decided to make a definitive turnover and return to my true vocation: software development.

### *The Turning Point: Rapid Ascent at Ford Motor Company*

Leveraging the wealth of free educational resources made available during the pandemic, I threw myself back into programming. I completed an Android development certification course on Udemy and studied Unity game engine resources. Within less than a year of intense, focused study, I built a small portfolio to showcase my capabilities. In December 2021, I was hired by Ford Motor Company as an Android Developer for Embedded Automotive Applications.
I entered the company as a scholarship Innovation Researcher while simultaneously beginning an Associate of Science degree in Big Data and Data Science. My hiring was a direct result of my raw potential, fluent English, and compelling background, which convinced the recruiting team to overlook the standard requirement of a formal degree or 5 years of prior experience.<br/>
Starting with significant financial pressure and working alongside younger, formally graduated peers, I utilized my competitive drive to stand out immediately:
- Embedded Systems Innovation: On my own initiative, I designed and developed a real-time log monitoring application specifically tailored for testing In-Vehicle Infotainment (IVI) systems. This solution streamlined internal validation workflows and caught critical bugs early in the cycle, leading directly to my full-time promotion as a Software Analyst within my very first year.
- Global Radio Signal generation: Moving into my second year, a colleague and I were entrusted with the comprehensive responsibility of overseeing embedded systems testing for Ford across Brazil, collaborating directly with the US-based corporate team. Recognizing an efficiency bottleneck in hardware validation, we co-developed and deployed a radio signal generation system using Raspberry Pi. Capable of testing global radio frequencies, this project earned me another promotion in just my second year.

### *Scale and Architecture: AI and Healthcare Foundations*

Following my success in the automotive sector, I transitioned into the fast-paced global startup ecosystem, joining a US-based AI Healthcare startup. My primary mandate was to scale an AI system designed to analyze and evaluate highly complex medical reports.
Dealing with highly unstructured medical data, the core engineering challenge was extracting clinical insights reliably while strictly eliminating LLM "hallucinations." To solve this, I built an AI orchestration pipeline using Python, FastAPI, and LangChain, integrated with the OpenAI API. I heavily utilized Function Calling combined with rigorous validation schemas through Pydantic and Zod, forcing the language models to deliver 100% structured data matching our strict business rules.<br/>
On the backend and infrastructure side, my contributions included:
- Developing secure, high-performance APIs using Node.js and TypeScript.
- Implementing robust caching layers utilizing Redis, which significantly reduced external API transactional costs and enhanced response times.
- Ensuring full compliance with HIPAA practices and OWASP safety guidelines by engineering strict data encryption, secure access controls via JWT and OAuth2, and resilient validation layers to protect sensitive patient privacy.
- Containerizing and deploying the cloud-native application on AWS using Docker and Kubernetes to guarantee seamless scalability.
<br/>
Originally brought in for a 3-month contract to design and kickstart the AI pipeline, the quality of my deliveries led to a contract extension to 6 months. This allowed me to fully deploy the infrastructure, secure the APIs, and stabilize the system successfully in production before the project reached its natural conclusion.
